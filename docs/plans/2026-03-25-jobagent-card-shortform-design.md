# JobAgent Card Shortform Design

**Date:** 2026-03-25

## Goal

Recast the first `Projects` card into a colder, more premium product voice without changing the card layout or data structure.

## Constraints

- Keep the existing card hierarchy and interaction model.
- Preserve the same project facts, stack, status, and CTA.
- Shorten copy aggressively without making the project sound vague.

## Approved Direction

- Shorten the title to feel more product-like than explanatory.
- Turn the description into a single controlled sentence.
- Replace the current impact line with tighter, colder capability labels.
- Compress the three delivery bullets into crisp, editorial product statements.

## Expected Result

- The first card reads faster.
- The tone feels more premium and deliberate.
- The card still communicates a full shipped system instead of a concept demo.
