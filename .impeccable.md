## Design Context

### Users
The primary audience is hiring managers and technical interviewers evaluating Fred Zhang for software engineering roles. They need to understand his positioning quickly, see credible evidence of technical depth, and decide whether to continue into projects, résumé, or contact.

### Brand Personality
Creative, technically credible, and intentional. The experience should position Fred as a creative developer who combines strong visual judgment with the ability to ship substantial, working products.

### Aesthetic Direction
An editorial white-and-blue portfolio with oversized typography, expressive motion, and a distinctive bird mark. It should feel visually confident and technically polished without obscuring project evidence, résumé access, or contact paths. Both light and dark themes are supported by the current implementation.

### Design Principles
1. Establish Fred's role, value, and technical credibility within the first screen.
2. Treat project outcomes, architecture, and shipped functionality as evidence—not decoration.
3. Use expressive typography and motion to demonstrate taste while preserving fast scanning and accessibility.
4. Make the next hiring action obvious: inspect work, open the résumé, or contact Fred.
5. Keep the experience distinctive and authored; avoid generic portfolio grids and ornamental effects without meaning.
